Mute Meeting

Mute and unmute your Google Meet calls on the Mac from your iPhone, Apple Watch, Control Center or AirPods Max. It presses Meet's own mute button, so everyone in the call sees you're muted.

What this is. Aghiad has built Mute Meeting and it works on his Mac, iPhone, Apple Watch and AirPods Max. It isn't in the App Store yet, so you can't install it: this page shows what it does. Read it, then tell us at the bottom what you think. Honest answers help most.

The problem

In a Google Meet call on a Mac, you can only mute from the keyboard or the mouse. If you step away from the desk, walk around during a call or work on a second screen, there's no quick way to mute. Apps that mute the whole Mac microphone exist, but then Meet still shows you as unmuted, and the remote can't tell whether you're muted.

Every way to mute

  • The big button on the iPhoneRed for muted, green for live, always the real state.
  • Control CenterOne tap, even with the iPhone locked.
  • Apple WatchTap, or double tap with two fingers. A different buzz for muted and live.
  • AirPods MaxPress the Digital Crown. A setting on the Mac. (AirPods Pro don't support this on the Mac yet.)
  • Siri and the Action Button"Mute with Mute Meeting".
  • Widgets and a watch complicationSee your mic at a glance, tap to switch.

How it works

Three small pieces: a free Mac app that lives in the menu bar, a free Chrome extension that presses Meet's button, and the iPhone app (with its Watch app). You pair the iPhone once by scanning a code. After that everything talks over your own Wi-Fi, encrypted.

Private by design: no account, no server, no ads, no tracking. The extension only sees whether you're in a call and whether you're muted, never the audio, video, chat or people.

Languages: English, German, French and Arabic. Price: paid once on the App Store, probably $1.99 to $2.99; the Mac app and extension are free.
